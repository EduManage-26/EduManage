Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password? expand_more
To reset your password, click on "Forgot Password" on the login page. You will receive a password reset link via email.
How do I add a new student? expand_more
School Admins can add new students by navigating to the Students page and clicking on "Add Student".
How do I contact my teacher? expand_more
Students can contact their teachers through the Chat feature in the student portal.
How do I add a new course? expand_more
Teachers can add new courses from the "My Courses" page by clicking on "Add Course".
